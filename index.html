Read the evidence

Did speculation soften this cycle?

This compares the root mean square (RMS) distance from equilibrium over matching, valid production years. A smaller value means prices stayed closer to equilibrium in those years; it does not prove long-term stability.

Wealth is cash plus inventory valued at the displayed market price. It is not realized profit or guaranteed liquidation proceeds.

Complete year-by-year data

Tables show all computed years; the selected year is highlighted. CSV includes full-precision observations, applied settings, and separate boundary records.

Year zero is the prior price used to determine the first year’s output. A dash means no valid observation, not zero. Signed trades: positive = buy into storage; negative = sell from storage. Inventory valuation is inventory × current price. Wealth adds cash; selling all inventory could change the price, so wealth is not guaranteed liquidation proceeds.

What this model assumes

One good, divisible units, one-year production lag, zero storage cost and interest, no borrowing or short selling. Trades clear at one price that includes their market impact. A run stops before negative production or a negative pre-trade price: this simplified model does not let speculation rescue an already infeasible year. A zero price is allowed.

The presets and clearing rule are decisions for this new browser adaptation. They show possibilities and limits, rather than promising that speculation always stabilizes markets.
